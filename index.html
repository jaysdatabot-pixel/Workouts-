Session 1

11:00 AM
Standing Extension ›
Stand tall, hands on lower back, gently lean backward through your hips. Hold 2-3 sec, return to neutral. 10 reps.
Diaphragmatic Breathing ›
Lying or seated, one hand on chest, one on belly. Breathe so the belly hand rises more than the chest hand. 10 slow breaths.
Quad Sets ›
Lie on back, towel rolled under knee. Push the back of your knee down into the towel — 2 fingers on inner thigh above kneecap to feel it fire. Heel stays on the floor. 10 sec hold x 10 reps, both legs.
Seated Sciatic Nerve Slider ›
Sit upright, feet flat. Left leg only. Slowly straighten the knee while pointing toes down, then bend the knee while pulling toes up. Slow, continuous, never hold the end position. 2 sets x 15 reps.
90/90 Hip & Ankle Work ›
Switches: In 90/90 position, rock hip to hip without using hands if possible. 8-10 reps.

Inside-ankle isometric lift: FRONT leg only (the one internally rotated). Press/lift the inside edge of that foot, hold 5 sec, 5-8 reps. Switch which leg is front and repeat.

Back-leg version is a bonus — front-leg inside lift is the priority.

Session 2

7:00 PM · right after dinner
Standing Extension (repeat) ›
Same as morning — quick set, 10 reps.
Standing Hip Hikes ›
Stand on a step, one foot fully on it, the other hanging off the edge. Let the hanging hip dip, then hike it back up like a shoulder shrug. Hold a rail for balance. 2 sets x 10-12 each side.
Clamshells + Standing Glute Squeezes ›
Clamshells: side-lying, knees bent, feet together, open top knee like a clamshell without rolling hips back. Glute squeezes: standing only (not seated), squeeze glutes hard, hold, release. 2 sets x 15 each.
Side-Lying Leg Raises ›
Side-lying, bottom leg bent, top leg straight. Lift toward the ceiling without rolling forward, lower slowly. 2 sets x 12-15 each side.
Single-Leg Balance ›
Stand on one leg near a counter for safety. Hold 20-30 sec each side. Progress by closing eyes briefly once stable.
Bridges ›
Lie on back, knees bent, feet flat. Squeeze glutes first, then lift hips into a straight line knees-to-shoulders. Hold 2-3 sec, lower slowly. 2 sets x 10, double-leg.
Sit-to-Stands ›
Sturdy chair, feet slightly wider than hips. Lean forward, stand without using hands if possible, sit back down slow and controlled. 2 sets x 8-10. (Not wall sits — avoid sustained lumbar load.)
Toe Yoga ›
Barefoot, foot flat. Lift just the big toe, other four stay down. Then reverse — lift the other four, big toe stays down. 10 reps each pattern, left foot (bunion side).
